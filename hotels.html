10 hotels, ranked by Michelin restaurants within 1 hour

Ranking = starred on-site → walkable (15 min) → 30-min → 60-min by RTC bus/Monorail/walk. Las Vegas has no subway; "subway or bus" = RTC Deuce bus (24h) + Monorail + walking. Bus times are estimates — confirm in the RTC trip planner ↗.

7-night total (2 adults): Nightly room rates are live/dynamic — there is no static published total to copy without inventing numbers. Click Book / check live total on each row for your exact dates. To compare fairly: (nightly rate + resort fee) × 7, then add ~13.38% lodging tax (Clark County Strip). MGM Grand resort fee $50/night + tax (published Oct 2026) is the only fee verified at research time; all others say "check live".

How the 1-hour counts work: From any Center Strip hotel, all 24 Michelin restaurants are reachable within ~60 min by bus/walk/rideshare — so the ranking differentiates by on-site + walkable density, not just the 60-min total. MGM Grand (South Strip) reaches 23/24 within 60 min (Anima by Edo, far SW, is the edge case). Downtown Circa reaches 24/24 but the Strip cluster is 35–50 min by Deuce. Chinatown (5 Selected + 1 Bib) is 30–45 min from Center Strip, 45–60 min from MGM Grand. Confirm any specific trip in the RTC planner before booking.
⚠ Booking tip: Book a refundable rate now (Nov 2026, ~3 months out) for Feb 19–26, then re-check prices monthly. Convention-week rates (MAGIC, IFA, March pile-up) only rise. Always compare the total with resort fee + tax — nightly-rate-only comparisons are misleading in Las Vegas.
