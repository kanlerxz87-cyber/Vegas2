Why the 1-hour rule does not rank these hotels

All 24 MICHELIN restaurants sit in the Strip / Downtown / Chinatown core, and every hotel in this table is in or next to that core. With no subway, the realistic modes are walking, the Strip Monorail, RTC buses and rideshare. No official door-to-door time was retrieved for any pair (F-02), so a "minutes to each restaurant" column would be invented.

What is verifiable is co-location: four restaurants share the Venetian/Palazzo resort, two are inside MGM Grand, two inside The Cosmopolitan. Those require zero transit. That is the ranking you see above.

Chinatown, Downtown and off-Strip restaurants (11 of 24) have no rail link at all — Monorail is Strip-only (F-17).
