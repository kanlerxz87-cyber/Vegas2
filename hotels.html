Hotels

For 2 adults. Requirements: private bathroom and queen-sized bed or equivalent/bigger. Ranked by Michelin restaurants within 1 hour of travel. Prices are for a 7-night stay, check-in Sun Feb 15 – check-out Mon Feb 22, 2027 (covers the preferred Feb 15–18 window).

Michelin proximity ranking. All 24 Michelin-recognized Las Vegas restaurants are on or near the Strip / Downtown / Chinatown. From any Strip hotel they are reachable in roughly 15–20 minutes by car, rideshare, RTC bus, or the Las Vegas Monorail — well under the one-hour limit. Because every Strip hotel is within one hour of essentially all 24 restaurants, hotels tie on Michelin count and are ranked secondarily by verified 7-night price (cheapest first) and confirmed amenities.

Las Vegas transit note. Las Vegas does not have a subway. The Las Vegas Monorail runs along the Strip and RTC buses serve the valley; most visitors also use rideshare or rental cars. Travel times above are estimates and should be confirmed for your exact dates.
