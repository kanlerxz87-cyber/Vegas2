Irregularities & notes

I-1 • Michelin guide vintage
The Las Vegas Michelin data reflects the inaugural 2026 Michelin Guide Southwest (ceremony Aug 26, 2026). One older third-party source (Sept 2025) incorrectly states Las Vegas had no active Michelin stars — that was superseded by the 2026 return. Used the 2026 guide as current (today is Nov 26, 2026). Source: Visit Las Vegas (official LVCVA).
I-2 • No subway in Las Vegas
The user asked about subway/bus travel. Las Vegas has no subway; transit is the Las Vegas Monorail (Strip) + RTC buses + rideshare/rental car. Noted on the Hotels and Activities pages.
I-3 • Hotel price comparability
The two verified prices are not apples-to-apples: Caesars Palace $226/nt is a standard room-type-assigned rate excluding taxes; The Venetian $6,170.43 is an all-in suite total including taxes and a high-demand night (Feb 18 $1,499.25). Clearly labeled; not merged into a single ranking.
I-4 • Dynamic pricing
Verified hotel prices are snapshots from Nov 26, 2026 for Feb 15–22, 2027 and will change. Venetian now shows $XXX — re-check live. Re-check before booking.
I-5 • Social links from third-party search
A few Instagram handles (e.g., @joelrobuchon.lasvegas, @ebyjoseandres) were found via web search, not confirmed on the official venue sites. YouTube/TikTok/IG search links added in Nov 27 update are generic search placeholders flagged unverified — do not treat as official until confirmed on each venue’s official site.
I-6 • Venetian calendar now shows $XXX
The Venetian official booking calendar for Feb 15–22, 2027 (fetched Nov 27, 2026) now shows $XXX for suite options — the earlier $6,170.43 all-in snapshot is retained as a dated snapshot but may no longer be live. Re-check on venetianlasvegas.com before booking. Dynamic pricing is expected for Presidents’ Day week.
I-7 • Restaurant total-for-2 estimates are not verified quotes
All “Total for 2 adults” values in restaurants.html are estimates based on price level ($–$$$$) and typical menu (tasting + supplements + drinks extra), not official menu totals. No Michelin Guide release publishes meal prices. Verify on each restaurant’s official menu before budgeting; starred tasting menus are most volatile.
I-8 • Guide MICHELIN vintage vs trip date
Michelin distinctions are from the 2026 Guide Southwest (awarded Aug 26, 2026). They are not a guarantee of 2027 status — stars/listings can change year to year. Use as current (today Nov 27, 2026) but confirm closer to trip.
