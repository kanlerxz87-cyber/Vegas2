Starred vs listed. A restaurant is starred only if it holds one or two Michelin stars (Joël Robuchon 2★; Guy Savoy, L’Atelier de Joël Robuchon, é by José Andrés 1★ each). Bib Gourmand (China Poblano, Esther’s Kitchen, YEN Viet Kitchen) recognizes great food at a moderate price — a Michelin distinction, not a star. All other listed restaurants are Recommended (in the guide, no star). Sources: official Michelin release • Visit Las Vegas (official LVCVA).
