MICHELIN restaurants in Las Vegas

24 distinctions from the 2026 MICHELIN Guide Southwest — stars, Bib Gourmand and listed restaurants kept strictly apart, with hours, published prices, official links and a social/video link for every row.

How tiers are shown. The official Las Vegas MICHELIN page lists 24 Las Vegas distinctions: 4 starred (5 stars), 3 Bib Gourmand, 17 Recommended. A star is a star. Bib Gourmand is the value category — it is not a star. Listed means the restaurant appears in the guide's recommended selection with no star. The official page's own wording for non-starred, non-Bib entries is "Recommended by MICHELIN Guide"; this site labels those rows "Listed in guide" and never calls them starred.

Captured 2026-11-27 from the official Las Vegas MICHELIN page. 2027 awards have not been announced (F-08).

Primary source: MICHELIN Guide Southwest 2026 — official press release, 31 Aug 2026 — the first-party MICHELIN press release, which lists every restaurant with its street address. Added by session 4d7b292f as the first-party primary source behind the LVCVA page. It confirms the ceremony was held at Fontainebleau Las Vegas, lists every starred and Recommended restaurant with its street address, and states the full Southwest selection is 64 restaurants across 24 cuisine types. The fifth starred restaurant, Monte, is in South Salt Lake, UTAH — not Las Vegas — which is why Las Vegas has 4 starred restaurants and 5 stars, not 5 starred restaurants.

Do not trust star badges on hotel sites. MGM's own Joël Robuchon page renders a "One MICHELIN Star" badge for Bardot Brasserie at ARIA, but Bardot's own page lists no MICHELIN award and it is absent from both official lists (F-24). ARIA is counted as one Listed restaurant, zero stars.

Food budget reality check

  • Only one total is published: Partage — 5 courses $130pp ($260 for two), 7 courses $160pp ($320), Signature $210pp ($420), before tax and tip.
  • Item-level only: Guy Savoy — à la carte $60–$290 per item (Colors of Caviar $120, Artichoke & Black Truffle Soup $100, Sea Bass $160, Marmite of Squab $200, A5 Wagyu $290).
  • Nothing published: the other 22 rows. Ask the venue or open the menu PDF; the site will not guess.
  • Dress codes are enforced: Joël Robuchon business formal, Guy Savoy formal, Wing Lei business casual.
