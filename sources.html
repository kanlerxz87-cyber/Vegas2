Overview / Sources

Sources and verification record

Every official page used, what it was used for, and what could not be retrieved. Capture date 2026-11-27.

Verification method. A value is shown only when an official source (hotel, restaurant, venue, transit agency, or the official Las Vegas MICHELIN list) states it. Each row on this site carries the link it came from. Where a value could not be confirmed, it is blank and appears on Flagged for review.

Capture date for everything marked "verified" here: 2026-11-27, except items explicitly labelled as logged by a prior session on 2026-11-26 (é by José Andrés and L'Atelier hours, the Venetian $6,170.43 figure, and the OPM holiday check).

Rest of the rules

  • Dynamic booking prices are snapshots. Every nightly rate was captured live on the date shown and will change.
  • No tax-inclusive total is computed anywhere. Booking engines display rates 'excluding taxes'; the tax amount is not published in the captured output, so it is left unstated.
  • Travel times are not modelled or estimated as facts. Where no official journey time was retrieved, the cell says so.
  • Michelin tiers follow the official 2026 MICHELIN Guide Southwest list published on visitlasvegas.com. 2027 awards have not been announced.
