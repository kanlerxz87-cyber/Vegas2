Timing notes that matter for Feb 15–18, 2027

Bellagio Conservatory

The Lunar New Year display runs Jan 9 – Mar 6, 2027, so your preferred dates are inside a live installation. The gardens go dark Mar 7–12, which matters only if you shift the trip later. Spring opens Mar 13.

Official show dates

Neon Museum in February

Open daily 3:00–11:00 PM with last entry at 10:00 PM in the Sept–May schedule. It is an outdoor boneyard: summer heat rules do not apply in February, but wind/lightning cancellations still can.

Official visit page

Mob Museum + Downtown

Open daily 9:00 AM–9:00 PM; The Underground stays open later. Pair it with the Neon Museum and a Downtown MICHELIN table (Esther's Kitchen, Soulbelly BBQ) to make one transit trip instead of three.

Official hours

Out-of-corridor trips

Hoover Dam, Red Rock Canyon and Valley of Fire need a car or a tour; no verified public-transit option exists. Decide Q-11 before choosing a hotel.
