Unresolved · confirm before booking

South Point resort fee and booking total do not line up cleanly

SynXis lists a $29.20 daily resort fee included in the prevailing rate and says taxes are not included. The hotel’s rate-calendar page says its resort fee is $33.00 per room/night; it also says posted rates include the fee and exclude applicable taxes. Separately, posted nightly King rates for Feb 15–21 sum to $918 while the seven-night SynXis quote says $919. The $1 mismatch and fee difference are not resolved here.

Ask South Point: “For two adults in one Premium Two Queens room, Feb 15–22, what is the final itemized total? Does the $989 quote include the full $33 nightly resort fee? What taxes and deposits are still due? Is a private bathroom part of this room?”

Transit · not end-to-end timed

Route 117 is timed to SSTT; the whole Strip journey is not

Route 117’s published South Point-to-SSTT leg is approximately 16–23 minutes on sampled runs; the Deuce frequency is posted, but the PDFs do not provide an exact travel time from SSTT to each hotel or attraction. Transfer waits, traffic, construction and the final walk add time. No under-one-hour claim is made.

Next step: use the RTC trip tools and live alerts for the actual weekday/weekend and destination closer to travel; budget for the transfer.

Decisions to settle

What should be answered before turning this into a booked plan?

  • Confirm exact arrival and departure dates; do you want the preferred three nights or a seven-night comparison?
  • Choose King versus two Queens, and confirm private bathroom, occupancy, non-smoking room and cancellation/deposit terms.
  • Get an itemized South Point fee-and-tax total directly from the hotel.
  • Pick a nightly dining budget and mark any reservation-only meals.
  • Decide which experiences need tickets now and which can remain flexible.
  • If staying at South Point, verify current RTC service and total route time for the destinations you expect to visit.
