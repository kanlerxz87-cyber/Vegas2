The date-sensitive bits

Keep these on the calendar.

  • Bellagio Conservatory: Lunar New Year display is listed Jan 9–Mar 6, 2027; the Conservatory is dark Mar 7–12; Spring display is listed Mar 13–May 15. Dates can change.
  • KÀ: the posted weekly schedule includes Monday–Wednesday and Saturday–Sunday performances, with additional dark dates. Feb 14, 2027 is listed as dark; check the exact date before planning around the show.
  • Fremont Street: free nightly entertainment is advertised, but a specific Feb–Mar 2027 lineup has not been verified.

Make the call

Three decisions will sharpen the shortlist.

  • Are the travel dates fixed, or can check-in move a day or two?
  • Would you rather pay more to stay nearer the Strip, or trade location for South Point’s larger room and lower verified quote?
  • Do you want a King bed or two Queens—and is a private bathroom a must-confirm booking filter?

Other open items—especially the South Point fee and some restaurant hours—are collected on the review page.
