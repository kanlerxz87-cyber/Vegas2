Dining shortlist · menu-first

Pick the experience. See what the menu actually says.

Stars and Bib Gourmand badges below are used only when the individual MICHELIN Guide entry verifies them. Where a venue’s own source explicitly says “Selected,” the card says Selected; other entries are labeled Guide-listed without inferring a Star, Bib or Recommended badge. All hours are current checks, not 2027 guarantees.

How to read the couple prices: A fixed tasting-menu subtotal is the posted per-person price × 2, before tax, drinks and tip. It is not an all-in meal bill. À-la-carte examples are item prices, not a two-person estimate. If a current official price was not captured, the price is left unstated.
