Read this first. Las Vegas has no subway; rail is the 7-station Las Vegas Monorail along the Strip only. A 1-hour bus/rail limit therefore cannot separate Strip hotels — every MICHELIN restaurant in the city sits inside the Strip/Downtown/Chinatown core. The hotel ranking below uses the measure that does separate them: how many MICHELIN restaurants are inside the same resort, i.e. zero transit.

Hotel prices are 7-night totals captured live on 2026-11-27 for Feb 15–22, 2027 and include each property's resort fee but exclude taxes, which no captured engine published.

Executive summary

  • The 2026 Las Vegas MICHELIN list has 24 distinctions: 4 starred (5 stars total), 3 Bib Gourmand, 17 Recommended.
  • Las Vegas has no subway/metro. Rail service is the 7-station Las Vegas Monorail plus RTC buses and the Deuce.
  • Every MICHELIN restaurant in Las Vegas is within the Strip/Downtown/Chinatown core; a 1-hour bus/rail filter therefore does not separate Strip hotels. Michelin restaurants inside the same resort is the filter that does.
  • The Venetian/Palazzo campus has four MICHELIN distinctions — the densest cluster — but its dated rate calendar returned no prices.
  • Cheapest verified 7-night total for a two-queen room: Flamingo Las Vegas, $581 including its $50/day resort fee, taxes excluded (captured 2026-11-27 for Feb 15–22, 2027).
  • South Point is the only property whose own engine posted a full 7-night total with fees for three different date windows: Feb 15–22 ($919 king / $989 two queens), Feb 22–Mar 1 ($979 / $1,049) and Mar 15–22 (no availability on Mar 20, 2027).
  • TIME-CRITICAL: Wynn's public Cyber Exclusive rate for Feb 15-22 2027 is $677 avg/night including the resort fee ($4,739 for 7 nights, pre-tax) versus $833 ($5,831) on the Flexible rate. It must be booked Nov 20 - Dec 4, 2026, and adds a $75/day resort credit up to $300 per stay. Verified saving: $1,092 over 7 nights.
  • The verified pre-tax 7-night price ladder now runs from $581 (Flamingo, 2 queens, 350 sq ft) through $812 (Horseshoe, king, 450 sq ft), $861 (Planet Hollywood, king, 430 sq ft), $989 (South Point, 2 queens, 500 sq ft), $1,421 (Paris, 2 queens), $1,498 (Caesars Palace, king), $1,940.47 (Resorts World) to $4,739 (Wynn, king, 640 sq ft).
  • On Resorts World's official February 2027 calendar, shifting check-in from Mon Feb 15 to Thu Feb 18 cuts the indicative 7-night room subtotal from $1,942 to $1,632 - about 16% - because Feb 16 and 17 are the month's priciest weeknights at $404. Feb 15 2027 is Washington's Birthday (OPM).
  • Two MICHELIN-starred restaurants at MGM Grand have complementary closures: Joel Robuchon is closed Mon/Sun, L'Atelier is closed Tue/Wed. Together they cover all 7 nights, but never both on one night.
  • MGM's own site shows a 'One MICHELIN Star' badge for Bardot Brasserie (ARIA) that Bardot's own page and both official MICHELIN lists do not support. ARIA is scored as one Listed restaurant, zero stars (F-24).
  • Red Rock Canyon requires a timed-entry vehicle reservation 8 AM-5 PM every day of the trip window (Oct 1-May 31) and charges $20 per car, not per person. Valley of Fire charges $15 per non-Nevada vehicle and officially warns that rideshare visitors can be stranded.
  • Hours coverage after the 2026-11-28 pass: 19 of 24 MICHELIN restaurants now have an official pages-stated weekly grid (up from 6), 1 is partial and 4 remain blank because the operator publishes none (Guy Savoy, Kame Omakase, Sparrow + Wolf, Yen Viet Kitchen). For activities, 9 of 15 have a verified grid, 2 are partial (Omega Mart publishes only 'Days and Times vary') and 4 are blank.
  • Official social accounts are attached only where the operator's own page links them: 5 of 24 restaurants carry one (Guy Savoy via Caesars Palace, Gymkhana, Anima by EDO and SoulBelly fully verified; é partial) and 6 of 15 activities do (High Roller via The LINQ, Neon Museum, Omega Mart, KÀ, SlotZilla and Hoover Dam). The other rows keep a YouTube search link, clearly labelled as a search and not an account. Three venue handles were checked and REMOVED because the official site does not link them.
  • Eleven of the 24 MICHELIN restaurants sit on no casino campus at all (Chinatown, Downtown, off-Strip). No hotel in this set puts more than four of them on the same campus, so the 1-hour rule is decided by bus/rideshare, not by hotel choice. The Venetian campus is the only one where four distinctions need zero transit.
  • Fremont Street Experience publishes no social handles on its official site and no operating hours for SlotZilla; both stay blank rather than guessed. Its own FAQ confirms SlotZilla needs about an hour end to end for a 30-second (Zip) or 1-minute (Zoom) flight.
  • Bellagio Conservatory is DARK 7–12 March 2027 between the Lunar New Year and Spring shows. Any itinerary in that window must drop it; the official 2027 show and dark dates are on the page.
