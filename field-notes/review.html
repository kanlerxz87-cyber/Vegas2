Open research flags

What still needs a direct check.

  1. Trip length: the three-night and seven-night scenarios are not interchangeable.

    Feb 15–18, 2027 is three nights. It is retained as the preferred exact-date rate reference; Feb 15–22 is the separate seven-night comparison. No 14-night hotel quote was captured.

    See both hotel scenarios · Read the seven-night outline

  2. South Point resort fee is inconsistent across direct sources.

    The booking engine displayed $29.20 per day, while the public hotel page stated $33 per night. A seven-night quote showed $919 before tax, but the final fee treatment could not be reconciled. Do not call this an all-in total until South Point confirms it.

    South Point dated search · South Point rate calendar · South Point hotel page

  3. Hotel quote bases are mixed; final all-in rankings are not defensible yet.

    Some prices include the resort fee and exclude tax, some provide a tax-and-fee-inclusive total, and South Point has a conflicting fee figure. Assigned-on-arrival rooms also do not guarantee a particular bed. Confirm the final checkout total, cancellation rules, and room name live.

    Review the rate labels beside each quote · Official searches: Resorts World, Wynn

  4. The Wynn three-night figure is a limited-time offer snapshot.

    The $774 average/night Cyber Exclusive display included the resort fee and excluded tax. The offer page showed a booking window of Nov 20–Dec 4, 2026 and travel through Jun 30, 2027. Availability, credits, and terms can change; this is not a general future rate.

    Wynn dated booking search · Offer terms

  5. 2027 MICHELIN distinctions have not been verified.

    The dining page uses the 2026 Guide Southwest selection listed by Visit Las Vegas: four starred restaurants (five stars total), three Bib Gourmand, and 17 Recommended. Keep those categories separate and do not carry the 2026 awards forward as 2027 results.

    Visit Las Vegas · 2026 selection list · MICHELIN Guide

  6. Restaurant meal totals, menu prices, and some weekly hours are still incomplete.

    Guy Savoy’s current menu lists dishes, not a two-person check. Several venues did not expose usable regular hours or prices in the reviewed official pages. Fixed-menu prices are shown only where directly published; à-la-carte items are not converted into a fictional average meal.

    Open the complete restaurant list · Guy Savoy official menu

  7. Michael Mina is in the 2026 list but is currently marked temporarily closed.

    The Bellagio official page showed a temporary closure when checked. Verify reopening and February service directly before including it in an itinerary.

    Michael Mina · official Bellagio status page

  8. Sphere: no exact Feb 15–18 show date or ticket price was confirmed.

    The official calendar listed a Metallica residency range from Jan 28 to Mar 13, 2027, which overlaps the trip window; that range alone does not confirm a performance on a specific night. The Wizard of Oz experience runtime is about 75 minutes, but a February date and price were not verified.

    Sphere official show calendar · Wizard of Oz experience details

  9. Fremont Street’s February 2027 event schedule was not available in the checked calendar.

    Free entry and free stage entertainment are listed, but future performances should not be assumed from a past calendar. Check the official listing closer to the trip.

    Fremont Street Experience calendar

  10. No complete hotel-to-venue transit-time matrix was verified.

    RTC schedules and GTFS resources support route planning, not a single door-to-door travel time. The one Google Maps example shown is explicitly a short walking result, not public transit. Use the route form with the actual travel date and stop.

    Open transit sources and route planner · RTC Maps & Guides

  11. “Best time to book” cannot be generalized from these rate snapshots.

    Promotions observed on Nov 26, 2026 have property-specific terms. They do not predict future rates or establish a universally optimal booking window; compare refundable and nonrefundable offers directly.

    Read the booking-timing note
