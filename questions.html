Questions, Information & Answers

What is answered (with sources), what is still missing, and exactly how to discover each gap. Status as of Nov 27, 2026.

Do not book yet on unverified cells. Blank cells are blank because they could not be confirmed from an official source. Nothing on this site should be treated as a confirmed price, hour, or availability until verified on the linked official page.

Answered (verified)

Q-1. Which Michelin restaurants are starred vs merely listed? answered
Answer: 4 starred — Joël Robuchon (2★), Guy Savoy, L’Atelier, é by José Andrés (1★ each). 3 Bib Gourmand (a distinction, not a star). 17 Recommended (no star). 2026 selection; not a guarantee of 2027 status.
Sources: official Michelin release • Visit Las Vegas (LVCVA)
Q-2. Is Feb 15, 2027 a holiday? answered
Answer: Yes — Monday, Feb 15, 2027 is Washington’s Birthday (Presidents’ Day), a federal holiday. Expect holiday/weekend demand pricing on the first nights.
Source: OPM federal holidays (prior verification Nov 26)
Q-3. Does Las Vegas have a subway? What transit exists? answered
Answer: No subway service is published on the official RTC or Monorail sites. Public transit = RTC buses (Deuce 24/7, ~every 15 min; Strip fares $4 / $6 / $8 / $20) + Las Vegas Monorail (7 Strip stations, trains every 4–8 min, separate tickets; Mon 7 AM–midnight, Tue–Thu 7 AM–2 AM, Fri–Sun 7 AM–3 AM). Fares/hours verified Nov 27, 2026 — re-check for Feb 2027.
Sources: RTC • lvmonorail.com
Q-4. Which hotels meet the queen-or-larger + private-bath requirement? mostly answered
Answer: Queen-or-larger beds verified at all 8 ranked hotels (Venetian, MGM Grand, Cosmopolitan, Caesars, Bellagio, Wynn, ARIA suite-only, Resorts World Conrad). Private bathrooms fully verified at Caesars, ARIA (suite), and Resorts World Conrad; partial (bath described, “private” not stated, or not confirmed for the 2-queen type) at the other five — confirm at booking. See Hotels.
Sources: per-row official room pages linked on the Hotels page
Q-5. What are the verified hotel totals for 2 adults? partially answered
Answer: Caesars Palace (Nov 27, 2026 snapshots): 3-night Feb 15–18 = $549 + tax ($183/nt incl. $55/day resort fee); 7-night Feb 15–22 = $1,582 + tax ($226/nt incl. $55/day fee). All other hotels unverified; the Venetian $6,170.43 figure is a stale Nov 26 snapshot. See Booking & Prices.
Sources: Caesars booking 7-nt • Caesars booking 3-nt

Q-8. Restaurant cost for 2 adults? 6 answered, 18 open
Answer: Fixed-menu totals verified for 6: China Poblano $120; Anima $170; Sparrow + Wolf $298 (+$156 with pairing); Stubborn Seed $320; Partage $260/$320/$420; Michael Mina $590++ (all pre-tax/tip). À-la-carte restaurants are blank by rule.

Q-12. Which hotels are within 1 hour of which restaurants/activities? open — no official source

Q-13. Cheapest time to book? open — needs price log
Answer: Unverifiable from snapshots. Observed signal only: Caesars’ Mon–Thu 3-night average ($183/nt) is below its Mon–Mon 7-night average ($226/nt) because weekends cost more.
Discover: Run the weekly price log (fixed time, both stays, all candidate hotels) for several weeks, then read the cheapest window from the log.
Q-14. Reservation lead times for starred restaurants? open
Answer: Per LVCVA, starred restaurants typically release tables 30–60 days ahead; é by José Andrés takes reservations up to three months ahead. Not confirmed on the restaurants’ own booking pages.
Discover: Confirm exact booking windows on each starred restaurant’s official reservations page once dates are set.

Decision rule. A booking decision is reasonable only after Q-6/Q-7 (dates, budget) are answered by the couple, and the remaining open items are either resolved from official sources or explicitly accepted as unverified risks.
