Questions, Information & Answers

Items that must be addressed before any booking decision can be reasonably made, based on what we already have and do not have. Each item includes how to discover the missing information.

Do not book yet. Several fields below are blank because they could not be confirmed from an official source. Nothing on this site should be treated as a confirmed price, hour, or availability until verified.

Open questions & required information

Q-1. Which exact travel dates?
Answer: Preferred window is February 15–18, 2027; acceptable range is February 15 – March 23, 2027 (1–2 weeks). Not yet finalized.
Discover: Ask the couple to confirm check-in/check-out and total nights.
Q-2. Total trip length (nights)?
Answer: Not confirmed. Hotel tables use a 7-night stay (Feb 15–22, 2027) for comparison only; the actual trip may be shorter.
Discover: Confirm number of nights; recompute hotel totals per property for the actual length.
Q-3. Confirmed hotel budget per night / total?
Answer: Not provided. Verified data so far: Caesars Palace $226/nt (+tax) and The Venetian $6,170.43 all-in for 7 nights (Feb 15–22, 2027). Other hotels unverified.
Discover: Ask for budget; fetch real-time quotes from official hotel booking pages for the confirmed dates.
Q-4. Room requirements: private bathroom + queen bed or bigger?
Answer: Required for both adults. Verified for Caesars Palace (Julius Deluxe, 1 King, private bath) and The Venetian (Luxury King Suite, private bath). Unverified for candidate hotels.
Discover: Confirm bed type and private bath on each candidate hotel’s official room page.
Q-5. Which Michelin restaurants to prioritize?
Answer: Four starred (Joël Robuchon 2★, Guy Savoy 1★, L’Atelier 1★, é by José Andrés 1★) plus 3 Bib Gourmand. Not yet selected.
Discover: Check each starred restaurant’s official site for reservation release policy (most release tables 30–60 days ahead).
Q-6. Restaurant cost for a couple of 2 adults?
Answer: Not confirmed. Only per-person price symbols ($$–$$$$) are shown for some restaurants; no verified total per couple.
Discover: Review published menus on official restaurant sites; add estimated per-couple ranges, flagged as estimates.
Q-7. Restaurant days open / hours / exceptions?
Answer: Verified for Joël Robuchon, é by José Andrés, L’Atelier, China Poblano, Bouchon, and Wing Lei. Blank/unverified for the rest.
Discover: Visit each restaurant’s official site; record hours, closed days, holidays, and exceptions.
Q-8. YouTube / TikTok / Instagram links for each restaurant & activity?
Answer: Partial leads only (e.g., Joël Robuchon IG @joelrobuchon.lasvegas; Wynn IG @wynnlasvegas). Most are blank and flagged unverified.
Discover: Search each venue’s official site and official social profiles; confirm handles before linking.
Q-9. Activity hours, open days, exceptions, and prices?
Answer: All blank/unverified on the Activities page (Bellagio Fountains, High Roller, Sphere, Fremont Street, Neon Museum, Cirque, Red Rock, Hoover Dam, Shark Reef, Monorail).
Discover: Visit each venue’s official site; record hours, closed days, exceptions, and ticket prices for 2 adults.
Q-10. Cheapest time to book the hotel?
Answer: Partially indicated — at The Venetian, Thu Feb 19 was cheapest ($374.25) vs Fri Feb 18 ($1,499.25); mid-week tends to be cheaper. Not confirmed across all hotels or the full Feb 15–Mar 23 window.
Discover: Pull rate calendars from official hotel booking pages for multiple dates in the window and compare.
Q-11. Transportation within one hour of hotel?
Answer: Las Vegas has no subway; options are the Las Vegas Monorail (Strip), RTC buses, rideshare, rental car. Not yet tied to a specific hotel.
Discover: Choose hotel first, then confirm monorail/bus stops and ride times to each activity/restaurant.
Q-12. Reservation lead time for starred restaurants?
Answer: Per Visit Las Vegas, starred restaurants typically release tables 30–60 days ahead; é by José Andrés takes reservations up to three months ahead, two seatings nightly.
Discover: Confirm exact booking windows on each starred restaurant’s official site once travel dates are set.

Suggestions to discover missing information

  • Open each restaurant’s official site (links on the Michelin Restaurants page) and record hours, closed days, exceptions, and menu prices.
  • Open each candidate hotel’s official booking page for the confirmed dates and record private bath, bed type, and the all-in 7-night total including resort fees and taxes.
  • For starred restaurants, check the reservation release policy (30–60 days ahead is typical) and book as soon as the window opens.
  • For activities, visit each venue’s official site and record hours, open days, exceptions, and ticket prices for 2 adults.
  • Confirm social-media handles (YouTube/TikTok/Instagram) from each venue’s official site before linking.
  • Re-check hotel prices close to booking, since rates are dynamic and the verified snapshots will go stale.

Decision rule. A booking decision is reasonable only after Q-1 through Q-3 (dates, length, budget) are answered and Q-4 through Q-12 are resolved or explicitly accepted as unverified risks.
