Reading the table. “Arithmetic total” multiplies the snapshot nightly rate by the number of nights. It is not a quoted total: hotels price each night separately, and weekend/holiday nights cost more (that is why Caesars’ 7-night average, $226/nt, exceeds its Mon–Thu 3-night average, $183/nt). Always click through to the official booking link for the itemized total with taxes.

Resort-fee conflict (flagged ROOT-8). Caesars’ room lines show “+$55 Daily Resort Fee” while its Policies section states “Resort fees: $62.30 ($54.95 plus $7.35 tax)”. Both are published on the same official page; this site does not merge them. Confirm the fee line at booking.

Caesars policies (official page, Nov 27, 2026). Check-in 4 PM, minimum age 21, government photo ID, credit card for incidentals; check-out 11 AM. Deposit $100/day. Parking surcharge (free for Caesars Rewards Platinum+). Dogs only, 2 max, 50 lb, fees apply.
