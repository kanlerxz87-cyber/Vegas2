Prices, dates and when to book

Live 7-night quotes for Feb 15–22, 2027, a second week for comparison, the March availability problem, and the verified booking-policy evidence behind the timing recommendation.

Cheapest verified option. Flamingo Las Vegas, Flamingo Room | 2 Queens: $581 for 7 nights (Feb 15–22, 2027), including a $50/day resort fee, before taxes.

Runner-up: South Point, Premium 2 Queens: $989 for the same seven nights including all fees, before taxes. $408 more at South Point for the week. Flamingo is center Strip; South Point is at the south end of the Las Vegas Strip.

These are snapshot rates from one capture. A rate is not a reservation, and taxes are excluded. Caesars Palace itself moved from $226 to $214 per night between 2026-11-26 and 2026-11-27 (F-23), so treat every total as a reading, not a price.

Is Feb 15–22 the cheapest time to go?

What the captures show. Among the windows actually captured, Feb 15–22, 2027 is cheaper than the following week at the only hotel that quoted both: South Point charges $989 vs $1,049 for a two-queen room (+$60, about 6%). The March 15–22 window could not be quoted at all because March 20, 2027 shows no availability.

What cannot be claimed. This is not proof that Feb 15–22 is the cheapest week in the whole window. Only one property produced comparable quotes across windows, and no historical or forward rate series was retrieved. Some March dates are already sold out, which is itself a reason not to wait.

Best time to book

No official source publishes an optimal booking lead time. What is verified: Caesars Entertainment advertises 'Advanced Purchase Savings — up to 30% off for stays 30+ days in advance' and free cancellation within 48 hours. Booking now (≈80 days before Feb 15, 2027) already satisfies the 30-day threshold.

Recommended action: Book a refundable/free-cancellation two-queen room now as a hedge, then recheck the same dated engines weekly and rebook if the rate falls. Cancellation terms must be read on the booking page — the 48-hour term was not fully retrieved.

Price-watch plan

  1. Hold a free-cancellation two-queen room now at Flamingo ($581/7 nights pre-tax) or South Point ($989) — whichever location you prefer.
  2. Re-open the exact dated links on this page weekly and record the total (S-08).
  3. If a cheaper total appears, rebook and cancel the hold inside its free-cancellation window — check the window on the rate, not on the marketing page.
