Is Feb 15–22 the cheapest time to go?

What the captures show. Among the windows actually captured, Feb 15–22, 2027 is cheaper than the following week at the only hotel that quoted both: South Point charges $989 vs $1,049 for a two-queen room (+$60, about 6%). The March 15–22 window could not be quoted at all because March 20, 2027 shows no availability.

What cannot be claimed. This is not proof that Feb 15–22 is the cheapest week in the whole window. Only one property produced comparable quotes across windows, and no historical or forward rate series was retrieved. Some March dates are already sold out, which is itself a reason not to wait.

Best time to book

TIME-CRITICAL, VERIFIED 2026-11-27: Wynn's public 'Cyber Exclusive' rate for the exact Feb 15–22 2027 stay is $677 avg/night including the resort fee, versus $833 avg/night on the same room's Flexible Getaway Rate. That offer must be BOOKED between November 20 and December 4, 2026. Booking inside that window saves $1,092 across 7 nights before tax and adds a $75/day resort credit (up to $300 per stay). This is the only verified, dated booking deadline found that changes the price of this specific trip. Separately, no official source publishes an optimal booking lead time, and Caesars advertises 'Advanced Purchase Savings — up to 30% off for stays 30+ days in advance', which booking now already satisfies.

Recommended action: Book a refundable/free-cancellation two-queen room now as a hedge, then recheck the same dated engines weekly and rebook if the rate falls. Cancellation terms must be read on the booking page — the 48-hour term was not fully retrieved.
