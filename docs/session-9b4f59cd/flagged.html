The Visit Las Vegas page says '24 distinctions' and names four Bib Gourmands, adding a fourth name not in the release. Both are official domains.

Impact: The Las Vegas total could be 24 (17 Recommended + 3 Bib + 4 stars) or the DMO's slightly different split. Star count is unaffected — both sources agree on 4 starred restaurants.
Next step: Treat the MICHELIN press release as authoritative for award class; treat the DMO list as a discovery aid.		 source

F-07 medium
method note		 Starred menus are priced per dish, not per person Guy Savoy and Partage quote dish or course prices. Multiplying by two diners gives a food-only subtotal, excluding tax, gratuity, wine and add-ons.

Impact: The $420–$2,080 Guy Savoy range is a scenario spread, not an expected bill. Presenting one number would be misleading.
Next step: Ask for the current tasting-menu price per person including service.		 source

F-10 medium
constraint		 Red Rock Canyon has no public transit and needs a timed-entry reservation Vehicle entry requires a timed-entry reservation 1 Oct–31 May, 8 AM–5 PM, booked through Recreation.gov. The RTC does not serve the canyon.

Impact: Any plan that assumes bus or monorail access to Red Rock is not viable. Transport must be a rental car or rideshare.

F-11 high
brief conflict		 There is no subway or metro in Las Vegas The brief asks for Michelin restaurants 'within 1 hour of travel by subway or bus'. Las Vegas has no subway or metro rail. Public options are the RTC bus network (including the Deuce on the Strip) and the privately operated Las Vegas Monorail.

Impact: All '1 hour by subway' analysis was re-scoped to bus + monorail. Distances that are not served by either are flagged rather than assumed.
Next step: Confirm whether car/rideshare time may substitute for the 1-hour transit rule.		 source

method note		 Derived totals are labelled, not presented as official Where a hotel published only a nightly rate, the 7-night figure is that rate × 7 and is marked 'derived'. Only South Point and Resorts World issued genuine multi-night totals.

Impact: Derived totals exclude taxes and resort-fee tax. Clark County room tax was not verified for these bookings, so no percentage is applied and no all-in figure is implied.
