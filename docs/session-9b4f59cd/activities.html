The transport reality

Las Vegas has no subway or metro. The brief asks for Michelin restaurants within an hour by subway or bus. There is no subway. Public options are the RTC bus network (including the Deuce on the Strip) and the privately operated Las Vegas Monorail. Anything not served by those two needs a car or rideshare. See F-11.
