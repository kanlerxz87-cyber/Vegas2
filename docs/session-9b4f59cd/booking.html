Which window is cheapest

Among windows tested with the same hotel and room, Feb 15–22, 2027 was the cheapest. Caesars Palace run-of-house rate: $209 (Feb 15–22) < $237 (Feb 15–Mar 1) < $248 (Feb 22–Mar 1) < $310 (Mar 15–22). Paris points the same way, on its cheapest published nightly rate per window: $203 (Feb 15–22) → $232 (Feb 15–Mar 1) → $233 (Mar 15–22). Mar 15–22 is the most expensive week inside the planning window.

  • Cheapest 7 nights, verified: Flamingo Las Vegas — Flamingo Room 2 Queens at $83/night, $581 derived for Feb 15–22, 2027, excluding taxes and resort-fee tax.
  • Cheapest 7 nights, hotel-issued: South Point — $989 for the same seven nights, issued as a single total by the hotel's own engine ('including all fees', taxes excluded). It is the lowest hotel-issued 7-night figure but not the lowest of all quotes.
  • Cheapest 14 nights: South Point — $2,039 for Feb 15 – Mar 1, 2027. This is the only genuine 14-night quote obtained, and it matches the '1–2 weeks' brief.

Totals marked 'derived' are the hotel's own displayed nightly rate multiplied by the number of nights. No tax rate has been assumed anywhere; taxes and resort-fee tax are excluded unless the hotel published an all-in figure (only Resorts World did).

The only documented timing lever is Caesars Entertainment's 'Advanced Purchase Savings — up to 30% off for stays 30+ days in advance', whose actual effect on these specific February 2027 dates is not stated by the company.

What would answer it: Re-run the same exact-date searches weekly and record the movement, or ask each property for its rate-hold and cancellation policy in writing. The Feb 15–22 week already prices below the March weeks, so waiting for a lower March rate is not supported by the data collected.

Fees, deposits and the fine print

Caesars properties: resort fee $56.63 (Flamingo, Paris) or $62.30 (Caesars Palace) including tax, plus a $50–$100/day incidental deposit. Resorts World: $55/night resort fee + tax. Wynn: $55/night resort fee + tax. South Point: $29.20/night resort fee, already inside the 'including all fees' total.

What to do next, in order

  1. Confirm the number of nights — 3, 7 or 14. Only South Point quoted a 14-night stay.
  2. Decide whether a car will be used; without one, Red Rock Canyon and Chinatown are not reachable.
  3. Re-price the shortlisted hotel on the exact dates and use the engine's Total Price toggle to see tax inclusive.
  4. Book starred restaurants as soon as dates are fixed — é by José Andrés seats nine and opens bookings three months ahead.
