How to discover the missing information

  • Ask the properties directly. Three hotels never returned a price. A single email each asking for an all-in quote for the exact dates resolves the biggest gap.
  • Use the booking engine's own total. Caesars and MGM engines both have a "Total Price" toggle that includes tax — that removes the need to derive anything.
  • Check the reservation widgets, not the menu pages. Most restaurants do not publish hours or prices, but their OpenTable or SevenRooms booking screen shows the service times they are actually selling.
  • Re-run the same search weekly. That is the only honest way to learn whether waiting lowers the price.
  • Ask RTC's trip planner for the specific pairs. It will give a door-to-door time for the actual date, which is the only thing that satisfies the one-hour rule.
