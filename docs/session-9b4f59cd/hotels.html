What the price column really means

  • Hotel-issued totals (South Point $989, Resorts World $2,434.84) come from the hotel's own engine as a multi-night sum. Resorts World's is fully itemised including tax.
  • Derived totals (Caesars $1,463, Paris $1,421, Flamingo $581) are the hotel's published nightly rate × 7. They exclude tax and resort-fee tax. The arithmetic is shown so it can be checked.
