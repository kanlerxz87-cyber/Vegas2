Repository review

Every finding below was read directly from the checked-out repository or the GitHub API on 2026-11-27; each row gives the file, the line or command, and what it shows. No finding is inferred.

Verdict. The repository is not broken — all 5 published session sites and the two root builds resolve their internal links (0 broken local refs across docs/**/*.html and 7 root pages). The problems are duplication, drift between published pages, and a deploy configuration that serves unchecked files.

R-01 high Published pages contradict each other on hotel pricing The Pages root page index.html line 58 states 'Caesars Palace $226/nt + tax ($1,582/7nts before tax) • The Venetian $6,170.43 all-in for 7 nts'. Today's live check of the same official engines for the same dates returns Caesars run-of-house at $209/night (→ $1,463) and The Venetian returning no price at all. Two different figures for the same hotel and week are live at once, and the Venetian figure cannot be reproduced today.

Impact: Two different all-in numbers for the same hotel and week are live at once; the Venetian figure cannot be reproduced today.
Fix: Re-price both hotels and publish one dated snapshot; state the capture date next to every price.		 index.html

Impact: A reader could plan around 15–20 minutes. The honest position is 'no published travel-time data'.
