Hoover Dam Hours Verified Fees Verified

Bureau of Reclamation · ~45 min southeast of Strip (driving)
Hours: V Dam: daily 5:00 AM–9:00 PM (PST). Visitor Center & tours: daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Doors close 4:15 PM; last tour 4:10 PM.
Fees: V Self-Guided Visitor Center $15; Guided Power Plant Tour $25; Guided Dam Tour $40 (on-site only). Children 3 & under free.
Exceptions: V Closed Thanksgiving & Christmas Day. Special events may limit access around Easter, early August, 3 days after Thanksgiving, and week after Christmas.
Reservations: V Visitor Center & Power Plant tours via official ticketing. Dam Tour on-site only.
Social: V Facebook · YouTube (Bureau) · Instagram (Bureau)
Link: Official tour info · Official home

Red Rock Canyon — Scenic Drive Hours Verified Fees Unverified

BLM · ~17 miles west of Strip (~30 min driving). No public transit to Red Rock.
Hours: V Oct: 6 AM–7 PM; Nov–Feb: 6 AM–5 PM; Mar: 6 AM–7 PM; Apr–Sep: 6 AM–8 PM. Visitor Center 8 AM–4:30 PM.
Fees: U Entry fee not confirmed on retrieved official page. Third-party guides say ~$20/vehicle + $2 reservation fee.
Exceptions: P Limited hours Thanksgiving (BLM 2025). BLM waived fees for Presidents' Day in 2021 (not confirmed for 2027).
Reservations: V Timed entry required Oct 1–May 31, 8 AM–5 PM. Walk-in & bicycle visitors exempt. Non-refundable. Recreation.gov
Social: P BLM agency-level: X · YouTube · Facebook · Instagram
Link: BLM official

Valley of Fire State Park Hours Verified Fees Verified

Nevada State Parks · ~50 miles northeast of Strip (~55 min driving)
Hours: V Open daily sunrise to sunset. Visitor Center daily 9 AM–4 PM.
Fees: V $10 Nevada-plated vehicle; $15 non-Nevada vehicle. Bike-in $2/bike.
Exceptions: V Annual maintenance closure Dec 1–14. Many trails closed May 15–Sep 30 (outside trip window).
Reservations: V Day-use: no reservation needed. Camping via reservenevada.com.

Link: Official page
