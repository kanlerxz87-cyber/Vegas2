Key finding — Michelin proximity. All 24 Michelin-recognized Las Vegas restaurants are on or near the Las Vegas Strip / Downtown / Chinatown. From any Strip hotel they are reachable in ~15–20 minutes by car, rideshare, RTC Deuce bus, or the Las Vegas Monorail — well under the one-hour travel limit. Hotels therefore tie on Michelin count and are ranked secondarily by verified 7-night price (cheapest first) and confirmed amenities (private bathroom, queen bed or larger).

Clusters (official addresses): Strip (Las Vegas Blvd) 13 • Spring Mountain / Chinatown 6 • Downtown / S. Main 2 • Off-Strip / W. Russell / S. Decatur / Gold Coast 3. See Michelin Restaurants for full list and Hotels for campus-based counts (Venetian 4, Cosmopolitan 2, MGM Grand 2).

Activities within 1 hr

All listed activities are within ~20–45 min of a Strip hotel by car/rideshare. Verified (official): Hoover Dam (daily 9 AM–5 PM tours, $15–$40 pp), Red Rock Canyon (Scenic Drive hours vary by month, timed entry Oct–May), Valley of Fire ($10 NV / $15 non-NV per vehicle), Neon Museum (daily 3–11 PM, from $21 pp), Monorail (7 stations, 7 AM–3 AM Fri–Sun).
Additional leads (Bellagio Fountains, High Roller, Sphere, Fremont St, Cirque, Shark Reef) are unverified — confirm on official sites.

Booking timing

Cheapest time to book: Feb 15–22 window is ~11–13 months out, so now (Nov 2026) is bookable. Dynamic pricing means no guaranteed cheapest day, but data shows mid-week > weekend/holiday: Venetian Thu Feb 19 ($374.25) was cheapest vs Feb 18 ($1,499.25). Caesars offers “Advanced Purchase Savings up to 30% off for stays 30+ days in advance” (terms apply).
Strategy: book refundable/flexible rate early for holiday window, then re-check closer to date; reserve starred restaurants 30–60 days ahead (é by José Andrés tickets 3 months ahead, two seatings 5:30 & 8:30 PM Tue–Sun).

About this site

Built as a static, mobile-friendly dashboard for the Vegas2 trip-planning project. Every value in a table carries a source link to an official site. If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified. No values are inferred or filled in manually.

Sources used: Visit Las Vegas (official LVCVA Michelin guide page), Michelin Guide official press release (Aug 26/31 2026), official restaurant sites (MGM Grand, Caesars, The Cosmopolitan, Wynn, The Venetian, ARIA), official hotel booking pages (The Venetian, Caesars Palace), official activity sites (USBR, BLM, Nevada State Parks, Neon Museum, LV Monorail), OPM federal holidays. See each subpage and the Flagged for Review page for per-row sources.

Note: this repository also contains prior sessions' work under docs/, data/, scripts/, field-notes/, legacy-dashboard/, and .github/workflows/. Those files are preserved and were not discarded during integration.
