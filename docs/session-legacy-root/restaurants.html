Michelin Restaurants

Las Vegas — 2026 Michelin Guide Southwest (awarded August 26, 2026). 24 distinctions: 4 starred, 3 Bib Gourmand, 17 Recommended. Status column shows whether the restaurant is starred in the guide or merely listed/recommended.

Starred vs listed. A restaurant is starred only if it holds one or two Michelin stars (Joël Robuchon 2★; Guy Savoy, L’Atelier de Joël Robuchon, é by José Andrés 1★ each). Bib Gourmand (China Poblano, Esther’s Kitchen, Yen Viet Kitchen) recognizes great food at a moderate price — it is a Michelin distinction but not a star. All other listed restaurants are Recommended (in the guide, no star). Source: Visit Las Vegas (official LVCVA).

Travel note. Las Vegas has no subway; the Las Vegas Monorail runs along the Strip and RTC buses serve the valley. All 24 Michelin restaurants are on or near the Strip / Downtown / Chinatown, so any Strip hotel places them within roughly 15–20 minutes by car, rideshare, bus, or monorail — under the one-hour limit.
