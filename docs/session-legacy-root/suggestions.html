Suggestions & Decision Paths

Suggestions for improving the site and the decision-making process. Review each suggestion and choose the best path before implementing any changes. None of these are implemented yet.

How to use this page. Each suggestion below lists the proposed change, why it helps, the effort level, and a recommended decision path. A reviewer should mark each as approve / revise / defer / reject before any work is scheduled.

Site-improvement suggestions

S-1 • Add a date picker & live price checker for hotels
Why: Hotel rates are dynamic; the verified prices here are snapshots (Feb 15–22, 2027) and will go stale. A date picker that calls each hotel’s official booking endpoint would let users compare real-time 7-night totals for their exact dates.
Effort: High (requires integration with hotel booking APIs or official site scraping, respecting terms of service).
Decision path: Approve only after confirming data sources & legal review; otherwise defer to a manual “check official site” link per hotel.
S-2 • Verify & complete missing hours, prices & social links
Why: Most restaurant and activity rows have blank hours/price/social fields flagged unverified. Completing them makes the tables decision-ready.
Effort: Medium (manual research of each official venue page).
Decision path: Approve — prioritize starred restaurants (4) and the user’s preferred dates first; track progress on the Flagged for Review page.
S-3 • Add a Michelin proximity map
Why: A map showing each hotel and the Michelin restaurants within one hour would make the “one-hour travel” rule visual and easier to compare than a table alone.
Effort: Medium (map library + geocoded restaurant/hotel coordinates).
Decision path: Approve as a Phase-2 enhancement after table data is verified.
S-4 • Add restaurant cost-per-couple estimates
Why: The user wants total price for a couple of 2 adults at Michelin restaurants. Current tables show per-person symbols ($$–$$$$) only. Add an estimated total range per restaurant (e.g., “$$$$ = ~$400–$700+/couple”) flagged as estimates, not verified quotes.
Effort: Low–Medium.
Decision path: Approve for starred/Bib Gourmand restaurants where menu prices are published on official sites; mark clearly as estimate.
S-5 • Add filters & sorting (star level, price, area, open-days)
Why: Helps the couple narrow choices quickly on mobile.
Effort: Low (client-side JavaScript).
Decision path: Approve — quick win, low risk.
S-6 • Add a printable trip itinerary export
Why: Users may want a one-page summary (hotel + dinner reservations + activities) to print or save to phone.
Effort: Low–Medium (client-side PDF or .ics export).
Decision path: Approve as Phase-2.
S-7 • Add accessibility & i18n improvements
Why: Mobile-friendly is required; also ensure WCAG contrast, keyboard navigation, and language options for international travelers.
Effort: Low–Medium.
Decision path: Approve accessibility fixes (already in CSS); defer i18n.
S-8 • Add user notes / saved selections
Why: Let the couple save a shortlist of hotels and restaurants and compare side by side.
Effort: Medium (localStorage or backend).
Decision path: Approve localStorage version first; evaluate backend only if needed.
