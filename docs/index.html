Executive summary

  • Dates. Preferred stay is 3 nights, Mon Feb 15 → Thu Feb 18, 2027. The full 7-night option is Feb 15 → Feb 22. Both are inside the Feb 15–Mar 23 window. Feb 15, 2027 is Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), a federal holiday. [OPM]
  • Michelin, Las Vegas 2026 (official). 24 restaurants: 4 starred (Restaurant de Joël Robuchon at 2 stars; é by José Andrés, Guy Savoy Las Vegas and L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon at 1 star each), 3 Bib Gourmand, 17 Recommended. [Michelin]
  • Michelin clusters. 13 of the 24 are on the Strip (Las Vegas Blvd), 6 on Spring Mountain (Chinatown), and 5 elsewhere (Downtown/S. Main, S. Decatur, W. Russell, Gold Coast). Counts use official addresses.
  • Best-placed hotels by Michelin count on campus. The Venetian (4), then The Cosmopolitan and MGM Grand (2 each). Caesars Palace, Bellagio, Wynn, ARIA and Resorts World have 1 each. This is a campus count only; the 1-hour transit test is not verified.
  • Rooms. Two queen beds are confirmed on official pages for Resorts World (Conrad, one king or two queens), Bellagio (Premier Two Queen) and the Cosmopolitan (two-queen listing). ARIA is confirmed for its Center Tower Suite only. Wynn's two-queen claim was withdrawn after re-checking the official booking page (see Flagged F17). Caesars Palace, MGM Grand and The Venetian remain unverified.
  • Price and booking time. No 2027 rate was verified for any hotel, so totals and the cheapest booking time are blank. This is the largest gap.
  • Activities. Hoover Dam, Red Rock Canyon (timed entry Oct–May), Valley of Fire ($10/$15 fee) and The Neon Museum: hours and rules from official pages. You have not yet named your activity list (see Questions).
  • Irregularities. Secondary press gives different Michelin star holders and Bib lists than the official release. The official release is used throughout. Full list on Flagged for Review.
