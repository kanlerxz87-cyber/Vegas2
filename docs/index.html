Executive Summary

  • Dates. Preferred stay: 3 nights (Mon Feb 15 → Thu Feb 18, 2027) or 7 nights (Feb 15 → Feb 22). Full window: Feb 15 – Mar 23, 2027 (1–2 weeks). Feb 15, 2027 = Presidents' Day (federal holiday). [OPM]
  • Michelin 2026 Southwest (official, Aug 26, 2026). 24 Las Vegas restaurants: 4 starred (Joël Robuchon at 2 stars; é by José Andrés, Guy Savoy, L'Atelier at 1 star each), 3 Bib Gourmand (China Poblano, Esther's Kitchen, YEN Viet Kitchen), 17 Recommended. [Michelin]
  • Best hotels by Michelin on campus. The Venetian (4), Cosmopolitan & MGM Grand (2 each), Caesars/Bellagio/ARIA/Wynn/Resorts World (1 each). All Strip hotels have RTC Deuce bus access within 1 hour to all 13 Strip Michelin restaurants.
  • Transit. Las Vegas has no subway. Strip transit is the RTC Deuce bus (24/7, every 10–15 min). Off-Strip restaurants (Chinatown, Downtown) require different routes or rideshare. [RTC]
  • Rooms. Queen beds verified on official pages: Cosmopolitan (Terrace Studio Two Queen), Bellagio (Premier Two Queen), Resorts World (Conrad). Others unverified. Private bathrooms unverified for all hotels.
  • Prices. No official Feb 2027 hotel rates verified. Michelin restaurant prices not in the official release. Third-party estimates exist but are flagged unverified.
  • Activities. Hoover Dam, Red Rock Canyon, Valley of Fire, and Neon Museum verified from official sources. Hours and fees confirmed where available.
  • Key gap. 2027 room rates, restaurant prices for 2, social media links, and transit times to off-Strip restaurants are the largest unverified areas.
