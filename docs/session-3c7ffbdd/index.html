2. Transit Reality Check (No Subway in Las Vegas)

Las Vegas has NO underground subway system. Public transit along the Strip and Downtown relies on the elevated Las Vegas Monorail (7 Strip stations) and RTC Southern Nevada buses (including the 24/7 Deuce on the Strip and east–west cross-town routes like Route 203 on Spring Mountain Rd and Route 202 on Flamingo Rd).

  • Las Vegas Monorail: 7 Strip stations (MGM Grand, Horseshoe/Paris, Flamingo/Caesars Palace, Harrah's/The LINQ, Boingo Station at Las Vegas Convention Center, Westgate, SAHARA Las Vegas). Runs Every 4–8 minutes; Mon 7:00 AM–12:00 AM; Tue–Thu 7:00 AM–2:00 AM; Fri–Sun 7:00 AM–3:00 AM. 7-day pass for 2 adults: $119.90 (eTickets) / $128.00 (full price) (Official Fares, @lvmonorail).
  • RTC Deuce on the Strip & All Access Buses: Approximately every 15 minutes (24/7 Strip service); Single Ride $4.00; 2-Hour $6.00; 24-Hour $8.00; 3-Day $20.00. Two 3-day passes: $40.00 (two 3-Day Strip & All Access passes) (RTC Fares).
  • Michelin Transit Access: Staying on Center Strip (LINQ, Flamingo, Horseshoe, Paris, Caesars Palace) confirms direct Strip/Monorail/Deuce corridor reach to 15 Michelin venues (13 Strip + 2 Arts District), with 6 Chinatown + 3 West/SW venues flagged for exact RTC evening bus verification.
