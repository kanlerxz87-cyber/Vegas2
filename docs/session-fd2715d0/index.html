03 / HOW TO READ THIS RESEARCH

Evidence labels matter

Confirmed

A specific statement appears in a linked official source (for example, MICHELIN award, official current hours, official ticket price).

Unverified

A live 2027 hotel quote, the selected room’s private-bath confirmation and inventory, a future opening schedule, an official social account, or a specific route time is missing. The cell stays blank and is explained on the review page.

No hotel is declared cheapest; no route is declared under one hour; no restaurant total for two is invented. Visit the Flagged for Review page before treating any blank as settled.
