Overview / Getting around

Transit · timed facts only

One bus transfer gets South Point onto the Strip network.

The current RTC timetable supports a Route 117 segment to the South Strip Transit Terminal (SSTT), then a Deuce transfer for Strip destinations. It does not support one reliable door-to-door travel time to every hotel or attraction.

Not an end-to-end arrival promise: Route 117’s published South Point-to-SSTT segment is roughly 16–23 minutes on the listed runs. Add the wait for Route 117, the transfer wait, the Deuce ride and traffic. No specific 2027 origin-to-destination trip duration is published here.

South Point → Strip

What is timed, and what is still unknown.

Board Route 117

South Point Hotel & Casino stop (RTC map stop D). The route connects the hotel area with SSTT.

Posted segment: about 16–23 minutes on sampled timetable runs. Some daytime runs are about 30 minutes apart; check the departure table for the intended day.

Transfer at SSTT

South Strip Transit Terminal is the interchange for the Deuce and multiple RTC routes.

Transfer time: depends on the connection. Route 117 and Deuce schedules are separate, so layover is additional.

Continue on the Deuce

The Deuce runs along Las Vegas Boulevard, serving Strip hotels and continuing toward Downtown/Fremont.

Current frequency: about every 10–15 minutes from 7 a.m.–1 a.m.; about every 20 minutes from 1–7 a.m. Ride time depends on the destination and traffic.

These are 2026 schedule facts, not a Feb–Mar 2027 guarantee. RTC lists the schedules as effective Aug 23, 2026.

Two systems, two different jobs

Bus for the South Point connection; Monorail for the east Strip.

RTC · Route 117 + Deuce

South Point ↔ Strip / Downtown

Route 117 serves South Point and SSTT. Transfer there to the Deuce for Las Vegas Boulevard, including Strip stops and Fremont Street Experience.

  • Route 117 South Point-to-SSTT scheduled segment: roughly 16–23 minutes on sampled trips.
  • Route 117 has roughly half-hourly daytime gaps for many of the posted runs; late service is less frequent.
  • Deuce frequency is approximate, and the timetable warns that traffic or disruptions can cause delays.
  • No exact total journey duration or total trip fare is claimed here.

Las Vegas Monorail

Strip stations & Convention Center

The Monorail has seven stations from the MGM Grand area to the SAHARA, with a stop at the Convention Center. It does not serve South Point or the west-side casinos directly.

  • Trains are listed every 4–8 minutes.
  • Station hours: Monday 7 a.m.–midnight; Tue–Thu to 2 a.m.; Fri–Sun to 3 a.m.
  • Use it for the east side of the Strip; compare walking distance from each station to the final venue.
