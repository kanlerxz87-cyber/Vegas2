Answered Questions

QuestionAnswerSource
Is Feb 15, 2027 a holiday? V Yes. Monday, Feb 15, 2027 is Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), a federal holiday. OPM
Which Las Vegas restaurants hold Michelin stars? V Restaurant de Joël Robuchon (2 stars); é by José Andrés, Guy Savoy, and L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon (1 star each). Michelin
How many Michelin restaurants in Las Vegas? V 24: 4 starred, 3 Bib Gourmand, 17 Recommended. Michelin
Does Las Vegas have a subway? V No. Public transit on the Strip is the RTC Deuce bus (24/7). No subway exists. RTC official
Does Red Rock Canyon need a timed-entry reservation? V Yes, Oct 1 – May 31, 8 AM – 5 PM. February is inside that window. Recreation.gov
