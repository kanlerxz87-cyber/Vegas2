Decision paths

PATH-A · Star-first, everything walkable

Pick: Stay at The Venetian Resort (4 MICHELIN distinctions on campus) or MGM Grand (both Robuchon rooms).

Pros: No transit needed for four MICHELIN rooms; suites are 650 sq ft+ with private oversized bathrooms; the Venetian is the densest cluster in the city.

Cons: Highest cost. No dated Venetian rate was retrievable, so the budget is unknown; a prior session logged $6,170.43 all-in for 7 nights.

Best if: The restaurants are the reason for the trip and the budget is flexible.

Still unknown: dated Venetian/MGM price, 2027 star status

PATH-B · Value-first on the Strip

Pick: Flamingo Las Vegas, Flamingo Room | 2 Queens — $581 for 7 nights before tax, center Strip.

Pros: Cheapest verified two-queen total found; center-Strip walk to Caesars Palace (Guy Savoy), Bellagio (Michael Mina) and the LINQ (High Roller); Monorail and Deuce on the doorstep.

Cons: No MICHELIN restaurant inside the resort; 350 sq ft rooms are the smallest verified in this set; two seasonal pools and a casino-floor location mean noise.

Best if: You want the 1–2 week stay and a restaurant list rather than a resort.

Still unknown: whether you accept 350 sq ft, resort-fee tolerance

PATH-C · Space and quiet, south of the noise

Pick: South Point, Premium 2 Queens — $989 for 7 nights including fees, 500 sq ft.

Pros: Best documented pricing across three date windows; largest verified non-suite room (500 sq ft); free valet parking; airport shuttle; 2,079 rooms.

Cons: Well south of the center Strip; public-transit door-to-door time unverified; no MICHELIN restaurant nearby.

Best if: You will rent a car, value space, and treat the Strip as a day trip.

Still unknown: resort-fee discrepancy ($29.20 engine vs $33 public page), car needed

What I would do first

  1. S-08 price-watch log — cheap, and it turns "best time to book" from a guess into a measurement.

  3. S-02 capture MGM/Venetian/Wynn rates — seven of eleven hotels are currently unpriced, which biases the ranking.
  4. S-05 transit matrix — the only way to honour your 1-hour rule with evidence.
