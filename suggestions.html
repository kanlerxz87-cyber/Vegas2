How to improve decision-making — options, not changes

Each suggestion lists what it is, why it helps, effort, and risk. Review, then choose (e.g. "implement S1 + S3"). No suggestion is built until you pick.

Site improvements

S1 · Live-price checker (highest impact)

What: One-click links that prefill hotel + dates (Feb 19–26) on official booking pages, plus a "record your quote" table (date checked, total, link) so totals become comparable over time.

Why: Solves the dynamic-pricing problem honestly — no invented totals, real quotes accumulate.

Effort: Low Risk: Low

S2 · Side-by-side hotel compare

What: Tick 2–3 hotels → compare Michelin counts, fees, amenities, and your recorded quotes in one table.

Why: Turns the ranked list into a decision.

Effort: Medium Risk: Low

S3 · Itinerary builder (7 nights)

What: Day-by-day planner (Feb 19–26) pre-filled with starred dinners + free activities, respecting closed days (é Tue–Sat, Cirque dark days) and ≤1-hr travel.

Why: Prevents double-booking a closed restaurant/show.

Effort: Medium Risk: Low

S4 · Map view (clusters)

What: Static cluster map (Strip / Chinatown / Downtown / Off-Strip) showing hotel + restaurant + activity pins with 15/30/60-min rings.

Why: Makes "1-hour" tangible at a glance.

Effort: Medium Risk: Medium (map tiles)

S5 · Budget estimator (for 2)

What: Inputs (hotel total, starred dinners, shows, transit) → total trip estimate with tax/tip toggles. Uses only recorded quotes + published menu prices.

Why: Answers "what will this trip cost?" without guessing.

Effort: Medium Risk: Low

S6 · Print / PDF trip sheet

What: One-page printable summary (dates, hotel, reservations, hours, transit) for the trip itself.

Why: Useful offline in Vegas.

Effort: Low Risk: Low

Trip improvements (decision options)

  • T1 · Shift dates to Feb 19–26 (recommended) — saves convention/holiday premium vs Feb 15 start. Trade-off: misses Presidents' Day weekend buzz.
  • T2 · Split stay (Strip + Downtown) — e.g. 4 nights Center Strip + 3 nights Downtown (Circa). Trade-off: unpacking cost vs Fremont/Arts District access.
  • T3 · One splurge + Bib/Selected balance — Joël Robuchon (2★) OR é (1★) as the single splurge; fill other nights with Bib/Selected (China Poblano, Esther's, Soulbelly). Trade-off: cost vs coverage.
  • T4 · Car/tour day — add Red Rock or Hoover Dam via tour (exceeds bus-only). Trade-off: cost vs the best scenery.
  • T5 · Show anchor — lock Sphere or Cirque "O" dates first, then build dinners around them (dark days vary).
