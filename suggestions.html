Suggestions & Decision Paths

Suggestions for improving the site and the decision-making process. Review each suggestion and choose the best path before implementing any changes. Status as of Nov 27, 2026.

How to use this page. Each suggestion lists the proposed change, why it helps, the effort level, and a recommended decision path. A reviewer should mark each as approve / revise / defer / reject before any work is scheduled. Items marked done were implemented Nov 27, 2026 and await reviewer sign-off; partial items are in progress.

Site-improvement suggestions

S-1 • Add a date picker & live price checker for hotels proposed
Why: Hotel rates are dynamic; the verified prices here are snapshots and will go stale. A date picker that calls each hotel’s official booking endpoint would let users compare real-time 7-night totals for their exact dates.
Effort: High (requires integration with hotel booking APIs or official site scraping, respecting terms of service).
Decision path: Approve only after confirming data sources & legal review; otherwise defer to the manual price log on Booking & Prices.
S-2 • Verify & complete missing hours, prices & social links partial — hours/prices mostly done, social open
Why: Most restaurant and activity rows had blank hours/price/social fields. Nov 27 update: hours verified for 19/24 restaurants and 10/12 activities; cost-for-2 totals added for 6 fixed-menu restaurants; all social cells remain blank (official-site links only — none found yet).
Effort: Medium (manual research of each official venue page).
Decision path: Approve continued work — next: venue-confirmed social handles + Sphere/KÀ February schedules. Track on the Flagged for Review page.
S-3 • Add a Michelin proximity map proposed — Phase 2
Why: A map showing each hotel and the on-campus Michelin restaurants would make the ranking visual. Note: a 1-hour travel-time map must NOT be drawn — no official transit-time source exists, and drawing one would be inference.
Effort: Medium (map library + coordinates from official addresses only).
Decision path: Approve as a Phase-2 enhancement showing on-campus counts only.
S-4 • Add restaurant cost-per-couple estimates done (verified-only version)
Why: The user wants total price for 2 adults. Implemented Nov 27 as verified-only totals (fixed-menu price × 2, pre-tax/tip) for 6 restaurants — no estimated ranges were added, because estimates would violate the no-inference rule.
Decision path: Reviewer to confirm the verified-only approach (no estimates) is acceptable, or explicitly approve labeled estimates.
S-5 • Add filters & sorting (star level, price, area, open-days) proposed
Why: Helps the couple narrow choices quickly on mobile.
Effort: Low (client-side JavaScript).
Decision path: Approve — quick win, low risk.
S-6 • Add a printable trip itinerary export proposed — Phase 2
Why: Users may want a one-page summary (hotel + dinner reservations + activities) to print or save to phone.
Effort: Low–Medium (client-side PDF or .ics export).
Decision path: Approve as Phase-2.
S-7 • Add accessibility & i18n improvements partial
Why: Mobile-friendly is required; also ensure WCAG contrast, keyboard navigation, and language options. Tables scroll horizontally on small screens; nav collapses to a toggle.
Effort: Low–Medium.
Decision path: Approve accessibility fixes; defer i18n.
S-8 • Add user notes / saved selections proposed
Why: Let the couple save a shortlist of hotels and restaurants and compare side by side.
Effort: Medium (localStorage or backend).
Decision path: Approve localStorage version first; evaluate backend only if needed.
S-9 • Weekly hotel price log for cheapest-window detection done
Why: Cheapest booking time cannot be found from snapshots. Implemented Nov 27 as Booking & Prices: dated 3-nt/7-nt snapshots plus a log template with the first three rows filled.
Decision path: Reviewer to sign off; assign a weekly owner for the log until booking.
S-10 • Dedicated Sources page + machine-readable verification record done
Why: Every claim needs a checkable source. Implemented Nov 27 as Sources plus data/session-c88d116c/verification.json in the repo.
Decision path: Reviewer to sign off.
